Shipping policy

  • Order processing: All orders are processed within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation.
  • Domestic shipping (India): We offer Free Shipping across all locations in India via reputed courier partners. Estimated delivery time is 2–5 business days depending on your pin code.
  • International shipping (Australia, Canada, UAE, UK, and USA): International shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on total package weight (Regular Tee: 400g, Oversized Tee: 500g, Hoodie: 700g) in Indian Rupees (INR). Estimated international delivery time is 7–14 business days. Any customs duties, import taxes, or local clearance fees charged by the destination country are borne by the customer.

  • Delays caused by courier partners, customs clearance, weather, or events beyond our control are not grounds for cancellation, though we will assist in tracking every order until delivery.

Cancellations, returns & refunds

  • Order cancellations: Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placement, provided they have not yet been dispatched. Contact us at petrocrib@gmail.com or +91 8129477575 with your payment reference to request cancellation.
  • Domestic returns (India — defective or wrong-size items only): Returns or replacements are accepted only for damaged items, manufacturing defects, wrong items sent, or items whose physical measurements differ from our published size chart.
  • 7-day claim window: To request a domestic return or replacement, contact us within 7 days of receiving your order with your order ID and clear photos/an unboxing video showing the defect or size-chart discrepancy.
  • Non-returnable domestic items: We do not accept returns, refunds, or exchanges for non-defective items, buyer's remorse, or sizing issues where the garment matches the measurements listed in our size chart. Please check our size guide carefully before ordering.
  • Restocking fee & domestic return shipping costs: A fixed restocking fee of ₹100 INR applies to approved returns. Return shipping costs within India are calculated based on parcel weight (400g–700g per garment); if a return is due to a verified manufacturing defect, transit damage, or wrong item/size shipped by us, return shipping is covered by Petrocrib.
  • International orders (no returns): All international orders shipped outside India are final sale. No returns, refunds, or exchanges are accepted for international orders.
  • Refund processing: Approved refunds (after deducting the ₹100 restocking fee and any applicable weight-based return shipping charges) are credited back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days via Razorpay. Approved replacements are dispatched at no extra cost.
