Cancellations, returns & refunds

  • Order cancellations: Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placement, provided they have not yet been dispatched. Contact us with your payment reference to request cancellation.
  • Domestic returns (India — defective or wrong items only): Because our apparel is printed to order, we accept returns or replacements only for damaged items, manufacturing defects, wrong items sent, or items whose physical measurements differ from our published size chart.
  • 7-day claim window: To request a domestic return or replacement, contact us within 7 days of receiving your order with your order ID and clear photos/an unboxing video showing the defect or measurement discrepancy.
  • Non-returnable items: We do not accept returns, refunds, or exchanges for non-defective items, buyer's remorse, or sizing issues where the garment matches the measurements stated in our size chart. Please check our size guide carefully before ordering.
  • Domestic return shipping costs: Return shipping costs for domestic orders are determined based on parcel weight. If a return is approved due to a verified manufacturing defect, transit damage, or wrong item/size shipped by us, return pickup/shipping is arranged or reimbursed by us; for any other approved weight-based return shipment, return courier charges correspond to the actual shipment weight.
  • International orders (no returns): All international orders shipped outside India are final sale. No returns, refunds, or exchanges are accepted for international orders.
  • Refund processing: Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days via Razorpay. Approved replacements are dispatched at no extra cost.
